# 1. Research Objectives

The primary objectives are to:

- Understand the challenges faced during animal rescue.
- Identify the needs of different stakeholders.
- Design features that simplify rescue coordination.
- Improve response times.
- Encourage volunteer participation.

---

# 2. Problem Statement

Animal rescue often depends on scattered communication channels such as:

- Phone calls
- WhatsApp groups
- Facebook posts
- Local contacts

These methods lead to:

- Delayed responses
- Duplicate rescue efforts
- Lack of visibility
- Poor coordination
- No centralized tracking

Sevana aims to provide one unified platform for reporting, coordinating, and monitoring rescue activities.

# 4. User Personas

## Persona 1 – Citizen

### Goal

Quickly report an injured or abandoned animal.

### Needs

- Simple reporting process
- GPS location
- Image upload
- Status updates

### Frustrations

- Doesn't know whom to contact.
- No feedback after reporting.
- Slow rescue response.

---

## Persona 2 – Volunteer

### Goal

Find nearby animals needing assistance.

### Needs

- Nearby reports
- Rescue status
- Navigation
- Achievement tracking

### Frustrations

- Duplicate rescue attempts.
- Poor communication.
- No recognition for contributions.

---

## Persona 3 – NGO Representative

### Goal

Coordinate rescue operations efficiently.

### Needs

- Verified reports
- Volunteer coordination
- Progress tracking
- Donation visibility

### Frustrations

- Incomplete information.
- Difficulty managing multiple requests.
- Lack of centralized communication.

---

## Persona 4 – Veterinarian

### Goal

Provide timely treatment.

### Needs

- Accurate location
- Animal condition
- Contact details
- Emergency prioritization

### Frustrations

- Missing information.
- Delayed communication.
- Unclear rescue status.

---

## Persona 5 – Administrator

### Goal

Maintain platform quality.

### Needs

- User management
- Report moderation
- Analytics
- Issue resolution

### Frustrations

- Spam reports
- Inappropriate content
- Lack of moderation tools

# 6. Pain Points

Current Challenges

- No centralized reporting
- Slow communication
- Difficult volunteer coordination
- No rescue progress tracking
- Limited NGO visibility
- Difficulty locating nearby veterinarians
- Low volunteer engagement
- Lack of recognition

Each Sevana module addresses one or more of these challenges.

# 8. Functional Requirements

The platform shall:

- Register users.
- Authenticate users.
- Allow animal reporting.
- Upload images.
- Store report locations.
- Display nearby reports.
- Assign rescue volunteers.
- Send notifications.
- Maintain rescue history.
- Award XP.
- Display leaderboards.
- List NGOs.
- List veterinarians.
- Support donations.

---

# 9. Non-Functional Requirements

The platform should be:

- Secure
- Reliable
- Responsive
- Scalable
- Easy to use
- Maintainable
- Modular
- Cross-platform compatible

---

# 10. UX Goals

The interface should be:

- Simple
- Fast
- Intuitive
- Accessible
- Mobile-friendly
- Consistent
- Minimal

Users should complete common tasks with as few steps as possible.

---

# 11. Accessibility

Design considerations include:

- Readable typography
- Sufficient color contrast
- Keyboard accessibility (where applicable)
- Screen reader compatibility
- Responsive layouts
- Clear error messages

Accessibility improvements should be incorporated as the project evolves.

# 12. Success Metrics

Key metrics include:

- Number of registered users
- Number of reports submitted
- Rescue completion rate
- Average rescue response time
- Volunteer participation
- Donation activity
- Active NGOs
- Active veterinarians
- User retention
- User satisfaction

---

# 13. Product Assumptions

The current design assumes that:

- Users are willing to report animals using a mobile device.
- Volunteers prefer location-based rescue requests.
- NGOs and veterinarians are interested in participating.
- Recognition (XP, badges, leaderboard) encourages engagement.
- A centralized platform improves rescue coordination.

These assumptions should be validated through user feedback as the project develops.

---

# 14. Future Research

Future research activities may include:

- Interviews with volunteers
- NGO feedback sessions
- Veterinarian surveys
- Citizen usability testing
- Rescue response analysis
- Feature usage analytics
- Accessibility testing
- Performance benchmarking

Insights from future research should inform future product iterations.

---

# Conclusion

Sevana is designed to solve real coordination challenges in animal rescue by connecting citizens, volunteers, NGOs, veterinarians, and administrators within a single platform.

User research should remain an ongoing process. As the platform grows, continuous feedback and observation should guide future design decisions and feature development.
