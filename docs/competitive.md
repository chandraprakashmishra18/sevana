# Purpose

This document analyzes existing platforms, identifies market gaps, and explains how Sevana differentiates itself.

The objectives are to:

- Understand the competitive landscape
- Identify strengths and weaknesses of existing solutions
- Define Sevana's Unique Value Proposition (UVP)
- Guide future product decisions

---

# Problem Statement

Every day, injured, abandoned, and distressed animals are reported by citizens.

Current challenges include:

- No centralized rescue platform
- Slow communication between citizens and rescuers
- Difficulty finding nearby veterinarians
- Limited NGO visibility
- Lack of volunteer coordination
- No reward or motivation system for volunteers
- Poor tracking of rescue progress

Sevana aims to solve these challenges through a unified digital platform.

# Strategic Positioning

Sevana positions itself as a community-driven animal rescue ecosystem rather than a single-purpose application.

By integrating reporting, rescue coordination, veterinary services, NGOs, and volunteer engagement, it provides a unified solution that addresses multiple stages of the rescue process.

# Conclusion

The competitive analysis indicates that while several platforms address individual aspects of animal welfare, there is a gap for a comprehensive, community-focused rescue platform.

Sevana aims to fill this gap by delivering an integrated, scalable, and user-centric ecosystem that supports citizens, volunteers, NGOs, veterinarians, and administrators through a single platform.
